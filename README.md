This is my personal portfolio website. It has three sections: About Me, Projects and Contact. It works on phones and computers and has a dark mode.

Design decisions:
1. On a computer, the projects are in three columns so I can see them all at once. On a phone, they go into one column because three columns would be too small to read.
2. I chose one purple-blue colour for buttons and links so the page looks simple and consistent. In dark mode I made the background dark blue and the text light.
3. I tested the page at 375px and changed the About Me section so the photo and text sit one under the other on a phone instead of side by side.
